How this works, in short

  1. Connect your Telegram account (once).
  2. Refresh the chat list below and pick a chat to manage.
  3. Choose which AI app may read it, and wait for everyone in the chat to consent.
  4. Activate. Only from that moment can an AI assistant read that one chat.

Demo mode. Adapter MOCK — all data below is synthetic. No Telegram account is connected.

Live adapter (TDLIB). A real Telegram account session is in use. Only chats you activate with recorded participant consent are ever read.
